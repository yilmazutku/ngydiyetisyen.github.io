Frequently Asked Questions

How can I register for the app?

The NGY Dietitian app is only available to registered clients. To register, please contact your dietitian.

I forgot my password, what should I do?

Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to receive a password reset link to your email address.

How can I upload meal photos?

Go to the "Meals" section from the main menu and tap the camera icon to take a photo or select one from your gallery.

How can I book an appointment?

From the "Appointments" section, you can view available time slots and select a suitable time to create an appointment.

Is the app free?

Yes, the app is free. Dietitian service fees are determined separately.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all your data is stored encrypted on secure servers and is not shared with third parties. For detailed information, please review our Privacy Policy.
